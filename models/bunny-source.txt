Stanford Bunny - official reduced-resolution meshes
Original authors: Greg Turk and Marc Levoy, Stanford University.
Source: https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/#bunny
Download: https://graphics.stanford.edu/pub/3Dscanrep/bunny.tar.gz
Downloaded: 2026-10-20
Converted from ASCII PLY to OBJ; vertex coordinates and triangle winding unchanged.

Default bunny.obj: copy of bunny-res3.obj (1889 vertices, 3851 triangles)
bunny-res2.obj: 8171 vertices, 16301 triangles
bunny-res3.obj: 1889 vertices, 3851 triangles
bunny-res4.obj: 453 vertices, 948 triangles
bunny-full.obj: previous downloaded full-resolution OBJ (35947 vertices, 69451 triangles)

To switch: copy the desired OBJ over bunny.obj, or pass its path as a command-line argument.
